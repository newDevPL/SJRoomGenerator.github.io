About The Plugin

SJ Procedural Room creates editable room shells from a closed spline. It generates walls, wall finish surfaces, floors, ceilings, roof variants, door and window openings, and editor baked StaticMesh output.

  • Included sample map: /SJProceduralRoom/SJProceduralRoom/DemoLevel.
  • Primary actor: ASJProceduralRoomActor.
  • Default bake output path: /Game/SJProceduralRoom/BakedRooms.

What The Guide Covers

  • Opening the included demo level and reviewing sample room layouts.
  • Editing room outlines, per-wall settings, materials, roofs, doors, and windows.
  • Understanding opening validation warnings and generated mesh lighting behavior.
  • Baking live generated geometry into StaticMesh assets.
  • Current limitations and validation performed for this documentation release.
